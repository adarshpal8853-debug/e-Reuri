ABOUT US

About Gram Panchayat Reuri

Meri Panchayat is a digital initiative for the residents of Gram Panchayat Reuri.

The platform provides citizens with important Panchayat information, public notices, government services, development updates and a direct way to submit complaints.

Our objective is to make Panchayat administration more transparent, accessible and responsive to citizens.
